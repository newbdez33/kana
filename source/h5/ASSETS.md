# Jumping Kana artwork

The rabbit and meadow are new artwork for Jumping Kana, created with the
built-in image generation tool on November 10, 2026. No artwork was copied
from Pyon. Pyon supplied the picture-book mood reference: cream paper, soft
green landscapes, and a friendly rabbit.

- `public/assets/bunny.png`: original cream rabbit, mint scarf, carrot
  satchel, transparent background. 1254 by 1254 pixels.
- `public/assets/meadow.png`: original watercolor meadow and stream with an
  open center. 1536 by 1024 pixels.
- `../../store/taptap/app-icon.png`: matching opaque rabbit icon, 1254 by
  1254 pixels.
- `public/assets/icon.svg`: a small code-drawn rabbit favicon.
- `kana.ttf`: the existing Kana typeface, copied by the build. This
  pre-existing font and the shared practice code are not newly exclusive art.

Prompts used:

1. A single original cream-white rabbit facing right, ready to hop, with long
   pink inner ears, tiny brown eyes, a mint-green scarf, and an orange carrot
   satchel. Gentle watercolor and gouache, readable at 90 pixels, transparent
   background, no words, ground, or additional characters.
2. A quiet watercolor meadow, cream sky, sage hills, daisies near the edges,
   open center, and a shallow blue stream in the lower third. No characters,
   text, logos, or stepping stones.
3. The same rabbit centered on a pale warm sage square background, with a
   small daisy and ample icon-safe margins. Opaque, no letters.

The complete prompts, original outputs, alpha checks, and checksums are
retained in the ignored `build/jumping-kana-art-20261110/` directory.
AI-assisted illustrations must be reflected in the TapTap content declaration.
No live generative AI service runs inside the game.
