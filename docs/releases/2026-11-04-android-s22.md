# Android 1.2.0 (5): Galaxy S22 Ultra acceptance

The final release passed portrait acceptance on 2026-11-04 before production
submission. The device was updated through Google Play internal testing. App
data was retained throughout the upgrade.

## Device and artifact

- Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra, model `SM-S9080`, Android 16 / API 36.
- Package `jp.jacky.kana`, version `1.2.0`, version code `5`.
- Installer: `com.android.vending`; original installation date: 2026-09-28.
- Source commit: `0d02f4ab02efeeaad62a52a5fd31564a10aec9e7`.
- AAB SHA-256: `658d5086d2afb44f41e1f4cbb08632565c8048a6d086e611ec731b38f98041da`.
- Internal testing release became available at 10:56 JST.
- Portrait acceptance only, as requested. Existing supported orientations and
  form factors were not changed. The device's automatic rotation was restored.

## Physical checks

- Upgrading retained 41 answers, a best streak of 34, and the coffee entitlement.
- Answered correctly through all five stages and reached a streak of 38.
- Reviewed portrait screenshots for the centered kana/romaji and final stage.
- Native vibration history recorded successful `TICK`, `CLICK`, and `HEAVY_CLICK`
  effects. The new record at 35 triggered the heavy effect.
- With haptics disabled, the next correct answer increased the streak from 36
  to 37 without adding an app vibration event. Haptics were then restored.
- Eight seconds on Home preserved the question and streak of 38 on return.
- Eight seconds with the menu open preserved the question, answer count, and best.
- The coffee sheet still displayed `Supported · Ads removed` after the upgrade.
- Force-stopping and reopening retained 79 answers and the new best streak of 38.
- The actual Android accessibility tree includes the prompt, streak, and stage.

Vibration logs confirm execution, not subjective strength. Audio resources and
playback paths were checked, but audible output was not independently assessed.
The device already owns ad removal, so a served free-tier banner was not verified
on this account. Ad-trigger and purchase/restore regressions passed separately;
production ad IDs and the billing implementation remain intact.

## Automated and package checks

The Android unit suite passed 59 tests. Final UI/localization and pixel alignment
checks passed, including eight kana/romaji prompts at two sizes. A test of the
actual Android accessibility tree passed after correcting the drawing order.
The optional store screenshot fixture was skipped. Release build, lint, and
bundle validation passed. The AAB contains all 18 feedback WAV files and the
production AdMob application and banner IDs. Play has the R8 mapping file; its
only release warning concerns native debug symbols from the bundled library.

Local evidence is under `build/android-s22-20261104/build5/`, with build and
instrumentation logs in the parent directory. The evidence is not committed.
Google Play confirmed `Changes in review` for production release
`1.2.0 (5) - Native practice feedback` at 11:03 JST. Its automated quick checks
were still running; the console will forward the submission when they pass.
Only this one production release was submitted. The existing full rollout and
automatic publication after approval remain in effect. No other app changes
were pending. Screenshot: `build5/play-production-review.jpg`.

[Google Play publishing overview](https://play.google.com/console/u/0/developers/4896965748454075126/app/4975495341700861726/publishing).
