Privacy policy

This policy covers Kana (五十音) for iOS and Android, and this website (kana.jacky.jp). The app's developer (Jie Zhang) is responsible for the data described here. Contact: newbdez33@gmail.com.

There is no account. Your practice history is stored only on your device.

1. Data on your device

Your answer statistics, recent response times, streak records and settings are stored on your device. We do not send them to our servers or use them to select ads. Deleting the app removes the local copy. If device backup is on, these data may be included in your Apple or Android backup and restored later. The backup provider handles those copies under your device settings.

2. Advertising

The question screen shows Google AdMob banner ads after an incorrect answer or a timeout. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can process IP addresses and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Where consent is required (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), a consent message on first launch lets you accept, refuse or manage purposes, and you can change your choice later from the app menu. You can change the iOS tracking permission in system settings. Refusing consent does not stop practice; you may then see non-personalised ads.

See how Google uses data from apps and Google's privacy policy. We do not attach your practice history to ad requests.

3. Usage statistics

The iOS app uses Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) to improve the app and measure advertising. It collects statistics such as an app instance ID, device type and OS, country or region and screen usage. We do not ask for your name or email address in the app.

The Android app does not include Firebase Analytics or a separate crash reporting service. Its practice statistics stay on the device, subject to the backup settings above. AdMob can still process the data described in section 2.

4. Ad-free purchase

The “Buy the developer a coffee” purchase removes ads. Apple's App Store handles iOS purchases, and Google Play handles Android purchases. The developer does not receive payment details such as card numbers. The app checks purchase status through the relevant store and stores the ad-free status on your device. It does not send purchase records or tokens to a developer server. Refunds follow the relevant store's terms.

5. Providers and protection

We use Google AdMob for advertising, Google Firebase for iOS statistics, Cloudflare for this website, and Apple or Google Play for purchases. These providers can process data in countries other than yours. Network transfers use HTTPS, but no storage or transfer method is completely secure.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict data, withdraw consent, object to processing or complain to a privacy authority. Contact us at the address above to make a request. Support email is used to handle your request and any related legal duties.

6. Children

The app is intended for a general audience learning Japanese and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

7. This website and policy changes

This website does not use advertising, analytics, tracking cookies or external fonts. Cloudflare receives the technical request information needed to deliver these pages. Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date; material changes that require notice or consent will be presented in the app.
