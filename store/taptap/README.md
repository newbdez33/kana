`promotion.html` composes the generated rabbit and meadow with HTML text.
Capture it at 1920 × 1080 or 1440 × 1440 to rebuild the promotion images.
The three screenshots are captures of the playable H5 interface, with local
sample practice records. The video shows correct answers, a meadow change,
correction, both kana charts, and pause/resume.

The illustrations use AI assistance; the game has no live generative AI feature.
See [artwork provenance](../../source/h5/ASSETS.md). These materials do not rename
the separate native Kana or Pyon products.
